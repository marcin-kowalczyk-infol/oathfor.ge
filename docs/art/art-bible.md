# Art Bible — direction v0.1

Status: accepted direction; character designs, palette values and export dimensions are not selected. Source: [ADR 0001](../decisions/0001-project-foundation.md).

## Visual direction

Dark heroic Slavic fantasy: monumental readable silhouettes, ancient forests, mist, fire, carved wood, stone, iron and ritual ornament. Serious and atmospheric, rewarding enough for daily use. No horror gore, cute-pet default or borrowed franchise designs.

Original lore may draw from Slavic folklore. Record historical references and distinguish regional tradition from our invention; do not present speculative reconstructions as settled history. **Local art/research rule.**

## Companion design requirements

Explore one distinctive guardian identity with four stages of progression. Preserve face/head structure, proportions and signature motif while changing equipment, material richness and effects. “Max” is a placeholder. The actual name, form and personality remain open.

The player must recognize the same character at small mobile sizes. Review silhouette, facial readability, contrast and UI text placement. **Local production acceptance criteria.**

## Before production

Select and record palette, materials, lighting, camera angle, proportions, transparency/background rules and canonical reference sheet. Reference the chosen artwork by stable asset IDs in the tracked manifest; masters stay in ignored graphics. **Local workflow: [pipeline](pipeline.md).**

Do not copy commercial characters or use ready-made game asset packs for progression/world art. Standard interface icons may use a reviewed consistent library. **Owner direction: [ADR 0001](../decisions/0001-project-foundation.md).**
