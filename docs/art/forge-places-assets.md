## Usage and limits

- Close-ups scale to the screen width, anchor at the top and fade into the page colour through a dark gradient. Text sits on the calm lower part.
- The flame crossfades four frames in a loop with a screen blend over the painted fire. It stops in the background, under Reduce Motion and while its screen is hidden.
- State seals are shown at 34 to 56 points next to a visible short label. They are hidden from accessibility because the label and the accessible description carry the state.
- Station poses: embers at the hearth, an open-hand presentation at the seals and reading at the chronicle. The seal-touch pose (second cell, row-major index 1) reads as a "stop" gesture and is unused.

Independent art review on 2026-09-26 found 10 of 11 exports usable. Notes: withdrawn and unresolved seals weaken below 32 points, the flame frames differ in height, the idle loop has a small height dip, the close-ups are upscaled about 1.18× on iPhone 18 Pro, and the sun medallion in the seals close-up resembles the fulfilled seal. The player character lineup and comparison are exploration material only and have no app export.
